## Tips for Delivery

### Before Demo:

1. **Test everything** - run race with friends beforehand
2. **Have backup video** - in case live demo fails
3. **Prep volunteers** - quick tutorial before presentation
4. **Screenshot stats** - from previous runs as backup

### During Demo:

1. **Narrate actively** - don't let silence happen
2. **Celebrate crashes** - "OH! Big collision!" (it's dramatic)
3. **Point to leaderboard** - "See it updating in real-time?"
4. **Have energy** - your excitement is contagious

### During Q&A:

**"How do you handle privacy concerns?"**

> "All data is anonymized. We track car IDs, not people. Users opt-in. Think Waze – same model."

**"What about system failures?"**

> "Graceful degradation. If the system fails, cars revert to normal driving. No coordination is worse, but not dangerous."

**"Why centralized vs decentralized?"**

> "Coordination requires a unified view. Decentralized means every car needs to talk to every other car – N² complexity. Centralized is N complexity and enables global optimization."

**"How do you deal with non-connected cars?"**

> "Mixed traffic is fine. We coordinate the connected cars around the unconnected ones. Even 20% penetration shows benefits."

**"Isn't this just vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) communication?"**

> "V2V is peer-to-peer. We're vehicle-to-infrastructure-to-vehicle (V2I2V). The infrastructure provides the intelligence and coordination that P2P can't achieve."

## Body Language & Delivery

✅ **DO:**

- Make eye contact with judges
- Use hand gestures to emphasize points
- Move around (don't stand still)
- Smile when showing demo
- Point at the screen when referencing data

❌ **DON'T:**

- Read from slides
- Turn your back to audience
- Speak in monotone
- Apologize for bugs (just fix and move on)
- Rush through the demo
